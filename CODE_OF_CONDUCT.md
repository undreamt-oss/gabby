# Code of Conduct

Gabby contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful, constructive community. We welcome
participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted
sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and disruptive or abusive conduct
in project spaces.

Participants asked to stop unacceptable behavior are expected to comply immediately. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other action proportionate to the conduct and its
impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through GitHub. Do not use public issues for
sensitive reports. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to
respond.
